Should Social Media be Restricted for Under 16s?

Australia's new laws restricting social media for under 16s have sparked debate. But what does the data say? This story uses personal screen time data alongside independent research to make the case that the restrictions are not only reasonable, they are necessary.

1. Social Media Use and Depression

More social media, more depression. The data doesn't lie, and your child is not immune.
(Ahmed & Syeda, 2023).

2. Stress Levels Increase with Screen Time

It doesn't matter which app. More time online means more stress, full stop.
(Dave, 2025).

3. Platform Design Drives Engagement

TikTok and Instagram aren't just popular, they're designed to consume your child's day.
(Personal Screen Time Data, 2026).

4. Notifications Do Not Fully Explain Usage

Even with few notifications, pickups stayed high. This isn't curiosity. It's compulsion.
(Personal Screen Time Data, 2026).
